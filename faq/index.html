No guessing

Straight answersabout Tawa Express.

Halal certification, dietary options, catering, hours and delivery, answered in plain words.

Is the food at Tawa Express halal, and who certifies it?

Yes, halal options are available across the menu, and the restaurant displays the halal badge in-house and online. Certification and supplier details change, so ask the counter for the current certificate or call (203) 290‑4673 and the team will confirm sourcing.

What is Indian fusion?

At Tawa Express it means Indian cuisine cooked alongside Indo-Chinese favorites and street food in one fast-casual kitchen: curries and biryanis next to tacos, 14-inch Indian pizzas, chaat and hakka noodles. Everything is made fresh, fast and full of flavor.

Do you have vegan and gluten-free options?

Yes. Vegan, gluten-free and halal options are available every day. The vegan cheese pizza at $16 is a specialty, and vegetarian curries like chana masala at $14 and dal makhani at $13 are menu staples. Ask the counter for today's gluten-free choices.

How does catering work?

Tawa Catering covers 50 to 10,000 guests: pickup trays from $80, buffet packages from $450, full-service events from $50 per person and live stations from $250. Share your event type, date and headcount through the quote form and the team prices it.

Why do hours differ across websites?

Older listings went stale: one directory still shows 11:00 AM to 10:00 PM daily. Our posted hours are 8:00 AM to 9:00 PM, and the restaurant is closed on Tuesdays per its Facebook notice. This page and our footer carry the current, correct hours.

Do you deliver?

Yes, through the live Tawa Express UberEats store, which handles pickup and delivery when available. You can also call (203) 290‑4673 to place a pickup order directly. Online prices on delivery apps run higher than the in-store menu prices listed here.
