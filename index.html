One kitchen, four cravings

What kind of food doesTawa Express serve?

Tawa Express is an Indian casual fusion restaurant. The menu runs from Indian street food and chaat to curries, biryanis, protein bowls, three-piece Indian tacos, 14-inch specialty Indian pizzas and Indo-Chinese favorites. Breakfast, lunch and dinner are all served daily at 3945 Main St in Bridgeport.

The halal promise

Is Tawa Express halal?

Yes. Halal options are available across the menu and the kitchen displays its halal badge in the restaurant and online. Vegan and gluten-free dishes are served too, so mixed groups can order together. For current sourcing details, ask the team at the counter or call (203) 290‑4673.
