For every diet

Which dishes are veganor gluten-free?

Vegan, gluten-free and halal options are all available here, and the menu was built so mixed groups can eat together. The vegan cheese pizza at $16 is a house specialty, and many curries and chaats can fit vegetarian diets. Ask the counter for today's vegan and gluten-free list.

  • Vegan cheese pizza, a 14-inch specialty at $16
  • Vegetarian curries: chana masala $14, dal makhani $13, baingan bharta $14
  • Halal options across the whole menu

Saturday and Sunday ritual

What's on the weekend thali?

On Saturday and Sunday from 11:00 AM to 3:00 PM, the kitchen serves thali platters: veg at $18, chicken at $20, fish at $22 and mutton at $22. A thali is a full Indian platter, a tasting of several dishes served together on one tray.
