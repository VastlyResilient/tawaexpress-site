Cooked in front of your guests

What is a live tandoor station?

A chef cooks fresh naan and tandoori dishes at your event in front of your guests. The full tandoor live station with oven, chef and helper is $900. Chaat and Indo-Chinese stations are also available; station setup with equipment starts at $250 plus food.

  • Tandoor station: oven, chef and helper, $900
  • Chaat station and Indo-Chinese station
  • Setup and equipment $250, chef $250 up to 40 guests, helper $150, plus food
