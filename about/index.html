Since 2003

Real food, real flavor,real value: theTawa Express story.

Meet Chef Jay and the Bridgeport kitchen that puts Indian street flavor on everything from tacos to 14-inch pizzas.

The founder

Who is Chef Jay?

Chef Jay founded Tawa Express and still leads the kitchen. He built the restaurant on one idea: great food should not come with a high price. Reviewers describe him as welcoming, genuine and clearly passionate, and he answers guest reviews personally.

His style, in his own words: blending authentic Indian street flavors with modern fusion, every dish made fresh, fast, and packed with bold taste.

The name

What is a tawa?

A tawa is the flat, round griddle at the heart of Indian street cooking. It sears breads, dosas and kebabs over high heat, which is where the name Tawa Express comes from: food cooked hot and fast on the griddle, served fresh.

One griddle.High heat.No shortcuts.

The philosophy

What does Indian fusionmean here?

It means the bold flavors of Indian cuisine meet Indo-Chinese favorites and classic street food in one fast-casual kitchen. In practice: butter chicken bowls, three-piece lamb tacos, 14-inch chatpata chicken pizza, samosa chaat and hakka noodles on one menu. Something for everyone.

The founding idea

Why does Tawa Expresskeep prices fair?

Because that was the founding idea. Chef Jay started Tawa Express in 2003 so great food would not come with a high price: real food, real flavor and real value. Today puffs still start at $2.75 and nothing on the dinner menu passes $22.
